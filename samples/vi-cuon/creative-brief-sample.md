## Sample 2 — Short Video Brief (TikTok / Reels — BTS Kitchen)

### brief_id
CB-VQ-20260528-002

### brand_id
VQ

### brand_name
Vị Cuốn

### asset_type
Video

### platform
TikTok

### format
9:16 vertical 1080x1920

### objective
Awareness

### concept
A 25–30 second BTS kitchen process video showing the daily freshness of Vị Cuốn. Three key scenes: fresh herbs being prepared, pork being sliced, and hand-rolling the final bánh tráng cuốn. ASMR-style audio. Builds trust through transparency — "you can see what goes in it." No narration required; on-screen text overlays carry the message.

### visual_direction
**Mood:** Warm, natural, honest — not polished to the point of feeling fake. Light and airy.
**Color palette:** Natural kitchen tones — green herbs, warm pork pink, white bánh tráng, wood chopping board. Avoid cold blue-white fluorescent feel.
**Lighting:** Natural window light preferred. If artificial, use warm-toned LED panel. No ring-light flatness.
**Camera movement:** Handheld or gimbal. Slight movement adds life — avoid static tripod feel for TikTok. Slow zoom-in on food texture moments.
**Audio:** ASMR-priority — water running, knife on board, bánh tráng crinkling, chopsticks. Background music (if used) must be TikTok copyright-safe.
**Text overlays:** Vietnamese. Short phrases. High contrast (white text with dark shadow on food backgrounds).

### scene_description
**Scene 1 (0–4s):** Close-up — fresh rau sống (herbs) rinsed under running water. Camera holds 3 seconds on water droplets on leaves. Text overlay: "Rau tươi mỗi sáng 🌿"

**Scene 2 (4–10s):** Medium shot — thịt heo luộc (boiled pork) being sliced on a wooden board. Soft steam rising. Knife sound is ASMR-forward. Text overlay: "Thịt tươi, không để qua ngày"

**Scene 3 (10–18s):** Close-up hands — rolling Bánh Tráng Cuốn Thịt Heo. Skilled, rhythmic movement. Camera stays on the rolling process — bánh tráng wrapper crinkle audible. Text overlay: "Cuốn từng cuốn tay 🤍"

**Scene 4 (18–24s):** Final dish reveal — plate of finished rolls with mắm nêm. Camera pulls back slowly. Text overlay: "Vị Cuốn — [OWNER_TO_PROVIDE_ADDRESS]"

**Scene 5 (24–28s):** Brand card — logo + CTA. Text overlay: "Ghé thử / Đặt qua inbox 📲"
