## Design Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use real product photography (no stock food images)
- Keep negative space clean — don't overcrowd
- Use brand colors consistently
- Show the actual dish clearly as hero element
- Add logo watermark to all published assets

### Don't
- Use dark or blurry photos
- Mix more than 3 fonts in one design
- Use neon or clashing colors
- Place text over busy backgrounds without contrast overlay
- Use unlicensed images or music
