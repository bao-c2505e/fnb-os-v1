## Content Don'ts
- Do not post blurry or dark food photos
- Do not use stock photos — all images must be real product or brand assets
- Do not make health claims that cannot be verified
- Do not post competitor mentions (even positively)
- Do not use copyrighted music without license

## Content Approval Flow
1. Agent generates content pack (caption + image brief)
2. Content pack written to Google Drive
3. Telegram approval request sent to user
4. User approves → scheduled for posting
5. User rejects → agent regenerates with feedback
