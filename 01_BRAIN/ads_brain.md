## Creative Rules

### Image Ads
- Minimum resolution: 1080x1080px
- Text overlay: max 20% of image area
- Must show actual product, not illustration
- Brand color border or logo watermark required

### Video Ads
- Duration: 6–15 seconds for feed, 30–60 for story
- First 3 seconds must hook without sound (captions required)
- End card: logo + CTA

### Copy Rules
- Headline: max 40 characters, benefit-first
- Body: max 125 characters
- CTA button: "Đặt ngay" / "Xem thêm" / "Nhắn tin"
